Document extraction — live OCR demo

A working recreation of my document-extraction pipeline, running fully in your browser — no server, nothing uploaded anywhere. Upload an image, a PDF, or a Word (.docx) document, type whatever text or pattern you want found, and for images/PDFs the matches get boxed directly on your original document (not just pulled out as plain text). You can also switch on the highlighter and mark anything by hand — click and drag over the document, just like a real highlighter pen — then download the result as an image or a PDF.

Image/PDF OCR: Tesseract.js. PDF page rendering: pdf.js. Word (.docx) text extraction: Mammoth.js — all running entirely client-side, your file never leaves your device. Row-matching logic mirrors document_extraction_pipeline.py, the actual Python script I built for this project (group OCR words into rows, test each row against the pattern, box the whole row) — extended here with a user-editable search pattern instead of one fixed pattern, and rendered live in the browser instead of saved to disk.
