About

From watching the dashboard to building it

I started the unglamorous way — as a Programmer Analyst Trainee at Cognizant in 2021, maintaining COBOL and JCL on mainframe systems most people my age had never touched. Three years later I was running production support for a healthcare platform serving 3.2 million members — monitoring alerts, coordinating fixes, administering the databases behind it. That job taught me what "production" actually means: no shortcuts, no guessing, someone's on the other end of every system.

Now I'm finishing a Master of IT at Monash while running four roles at once — technical, operational, and leadership — because I'd rather learn by doing than wait for a syllabus to catch up. That includes mentoring postgrad students, advising 45+ undergrad and postgraduate students, and administering IT systems for two different organisations, alongside cloud and data projects I build on my own time.

What I actually bring: production-grade discipline from three years of keeping real systems live, cloud builds you can inspect rather than tutorials I followed, and people-facing leadership experience most technical candidates my level don't have yet. Cloud and systems engineering is where I'm strongest — but I show up fully for data and software work too.

Solo Personal build

Full IELTS mock test practice tool

Role: sole builder

Built for my own IELTS prep — a browser-based mock exam covering all four sections (Listening, Reading, Writing, Speaking), because paid mock-test platforms were expensive and I wanted something I could rerun as many times as I needed.

  • Listening and Reading auto-score against the official IELTS raw-score-to-band conversion tables, with a live results breakdown by question type (form completion, multiple choice, map labelling, matching).
  • Listening plays each transcript aloud using the browser's built-in text-to-speech, with adjustable playback speed — no audio files to host.
  • Writing gives timed Task 1 & 2 practice with live word count; Speaking has per-part countdown timers plus an in-browser voice recorder to play back your own spoken answers.
  • A Feedback dashboard aggregates results across sections, flags strong vs. weak question types with targeted tips, and tracks average band against a target score.
