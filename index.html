Solo Data analysis

What actually predicts an independent artist's success

Role: sole analyst

Analysis of 114,000 real Spotify tracks (Kaggle dataset) to test which factors — genre, audio features, artist reputation — actually predict an independent artist's success on the platform. A topic I chose myself: first proposed as a business case study for a Monash assignment, then demonstrated with a real R Markdown analysis against the full dataset.

  • Wrote the R Markdown analysis myself: profiled the real 114K-track dataset and compared genre, individual audio features, and artist reputation as predictors of track popularity.
  • Found genre alone carried roughly 26.8x more predictive weight than any single audio feature (every audio-feature correlation to popularity came in below |r| = 0.1). That's the headline finding.
  • Also confirmed algorithmic gatekeeping in the data (under 1% of tracks reach "hit" status) and that artist reputation, not audio quality, drives repeat success.
